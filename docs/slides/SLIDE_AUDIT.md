## Checks performed

- compiled with `build.sh`; every page rendered to `render/slide-NN.png` and inspected as a contact sheet and at 110 dpi for slides 1, 3, 5, 6, 11, 12, 13; overlaps and overflows fixed on the figure side (label rows, clipped bars, strip captions) and on the LaTeX side (two-line tikz nodes as tabulars, figure widths, callout sizes);
- smallest type: 7.5 pt in figures and 7 pt for the two footnote lines (slides 1 and 12) on a 16 × 9 cm page, i.e. about 2.4 % of the slide height, readable at projection size; body 8.5 to 10 pt; titles 14.5 pt Lato Black; numbers 13 to 30 pt;
- the same five sensor colours on every slide (`figstyle.py` values in `style.tex`); the same depth silhouette on slides 1, 2 and 4;
- no bullet list is the dominant object of any slide; the only lists are the three tiny facts on slide 2 and the backup B7 package description;
- numbers cross-checked: every value on slides 3 to 12 is either a `\n…` macro from `gen/numbers.tex` or drawn by the figure script from the same table; the report's `FIGURE_AUDIT.md` holds the experiment ids;
- claims guarded: resolution is crop-dependent (slide 5); "0/405" is argmax stability with drift stated (12); LOSO is not the organisers' held-out set (7); primary reproduction 403/405 disclosed (12, B8);
- 3-second test on the renders: slide 1 reads FIVE SENSORS → ONE DECISION with the person in the centre; slide 3 reads "different sensors rescue different mistakes" from the coloured stripe and the 2,399 / 2,700; slide 6 reads the three thermal images and 0.544 → 0.627; slide 10 reads red left, blue right, "the bottleneck was not simply model capacity"; slide 13 reads the four numbers and the title.

## Known limitation of this build

`texlive-luatex` is not installed on this machine, so `build.sh` fell back to pdflatex with Helvetica for the slide text (the figures are Lato). Install it (`sudo apt install texlive-luatex`) and rebuild to get Lato throughout; the layout was sized with margin for the slightly different metrics, but the contact sheet should be re-checked after the first Lato build.
